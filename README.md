# OptimumWrap — Paint Protection Film & Vinyl Wrap

Factory-direct film supply for wrap shops, distributors and automotive film brands.

OptimumWrap manufactures Clear PPF, Color PPF and Vinyl Wrap. We help businesses explore colors, evaluate samples and source films for customer projects, wholesale distribution and branded product ranges.

Browse our collections, preview colors on a vehicle, or contact our team about custom colors and OEM supply.

## Explore Our Films

| Product range | What you can explore |
| --- | --- |
| [Clear PPF](https://www.optimumwrap.com/clear-ppf/) | Transparent paint protection film options, including gloss and matte appearances, for projects that retain the original paint color. |
| [Color PPF](https://www.optimumwrap.com/color-ppf/) | Colored paint protection film in a wide range of shades and finishes, with individual product details and sample inquiries. |
| [Vinyl Wrap](https://www.optimumwrap.com/vinyl-wrap/) | Car wrap films for color-change projects, with colors, finishes and patterns for different vehicle styles. |

Color PPF and Vinyl Wrap have separate product specifications, even when they share a color name. Select both the appearance and the film type when requesting samples or a quotation.

## Preview Wrap Colors

### 3D Wrap Visualizer

[Try the OptimumWrap 3D Visualizer](https://www.optimumwrap.com/3d-visualizer/)

Explore colors on an interactive vehicle model. Rotate the vehicle, zoom in and change the lighting background to view the overall appearance from different angles.

Use it during customer consultations or when shortlisting colors for a project.

### Color Preview

[Explore OptimumWrap Color Preview](https://www.optimumwrap.com/color-preview/)

Browse vehicle photo references and find the corresponding Color PPF or Vinyl Wrap product. Compare different looks before choosing which physical samples to evaluate.

Digital previews are appearance references. Screens, lighting and vehicle images can affect how a color looks. Confirm the shade and finish with a physical sample of the selected film.
